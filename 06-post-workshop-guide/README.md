# 06 · Post-workshop guide

> ⚠️ AI-generated · Reviewed by Haojun Yong (OTG).

You've finished CW-1 or CW-2. Now what?

## The next 7 days

- **Re-read your `you.md` / `brand-brief.md`.** Edit anything that came out clearer in the room than what you'd written. Save.
- **Run one of your three workflows.** Don't refactor. Don't redesign. Just run the inbox triage, the meeting prep, or the weekly summary you set up. Notice what's annoying. Tweak the prompt; not the schedule.
- **Push one update to your CW-2 site.** Something small — a typo, a better headline, a new blog post. Confirm the GitHub → Vercel auto-deploy still works the way it did in the room.
- **Join the alumni Discord.** Say hi. Share the URL of your site or the workflow you set up.
- **Update your aigowhere.com listing.** If you didn't finish it during the workshop, finish it now. This is your distribution.

## The next 30 days

- **Pick one workflow you don't do today and make it routine.** Half the value of CW-1 is the workflows you don't yet have but should. Long research on a topic you're curious about. A weekly LinkedIn post draft. Quarterly review of your customer list.
- **Publish 4 blog posts (CW-2).** One per week. Use the [code-prompts](../01-first-prompts-cheatsheet/code-prompts.md) you have. Don't aim for perfection; aim for "lives on the internet, indexed, citable."
- **Run Lighthouse on your CW-2 site.** Fix anything below 90.
- **Submit your sitemap to Bing Webmaster Tools** in addition to Google Search Console.
- **Show up to the Friday webinar.** It's the easiest way to keep the rhythm.

## The next 90 days

- **Audit which prompts you actually use.** Drop the ones you don't. Tighten the ones you do.
- **Build one custom thing** — a Claude project for a specific recurring deliverable, a CW-2-stack landing page for a new offer, a knowledge base file for a domain you keep working on. Pick one. Ship it. Don't get fancy.
- **If you want corporate training for your team, write in.** CW-1 and CW-2 are both available as private team sessions; we'll customise.
- **If you're an NGO or charity that didn't make it to CW-1 / CW-2,** apply for the CSR session. Free for up to 15 people.

## Things people stop doing too early (don't)

- Tweaking the `you.md` / `brand-brief.md`. It compounds. Update it whenever something changes.
- Using the same 3–5 starter prompts. The whole point was these are the foundations; build on them.
- Pushing code yourself instead of asking Claude. Claude Code is faster. Use it.
- Checking Search Console. Once a week is the minimum.

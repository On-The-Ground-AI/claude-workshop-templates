# Namecheap account setup

> Time: ~5 minutes. You'll need: an email address. CW-2 attendees only.

Namecheap is the domain registrar we use to buy and manage your custom domain. **We teach Namecheap + Vercel only.** If your domain is at GoDaddy, Cloudflare, or another registrar, the principles transfer but we won't be teaching the specific steps in the room.

## Steps

1. Go to [https://www.namecheap.com/myaccount/signup/](https://www.namecheap.com/myaccount/signup/).

   <!-- SCREENSHOT: namecheap-signup.png -->

2. Create your account: username, password, email.

3. Verify your email.

4. Skip the "find a domain" search — we'll do that in the workshop.

## Optional: get your domain ahead of time

If you already know what domain you want, you can buy it before the workshop. We'll cover this in CW-2 step-by-step, but doing it ahead saves room time.

- `.com`, `.co`, `.io`, `.sg`, `.ai` are common picks.
- Typical price: US$8–60/year (`.ai` is more expensive; `.com` is the safest).
- **Skip Domain Privacy add-on** decisions for now — Namecheap includes basic privacy free.

## What we'll do in the workshop

- Buy a domain (if you don't have one).
- Point Namecheap → Vercel via custom nameservers.
- Verify the domain on Vercel and let SSL issue automatically.
- Test the live URL from your phone.

## Don't worry about

- Email hosting through Namecheap — out of scope.
- DNS records you don't recognise (TXT, MX, etc.) — we'll touch them only if needed.

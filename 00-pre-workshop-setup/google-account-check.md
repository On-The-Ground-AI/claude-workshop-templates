# Google account check

> Time: ~2 minutes. You'll need: the Google account you actually use for work. CW-1 attendees only.

CW-1 connects Claude.ai to your Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive via Claude's connectors. To do that on the day, you need to know which Google account holds your real work data.

## Steps

1. Check which Google account is signed in at [https://mail.google.com](https://mail.google.com). This should be the inbox you actually triage. If you have multiple Google accounts, pick the one for work.

2. Confirm you have access to [https://calendar.google.com](https://calendar.google.com) (same account).

3. Confirm you have access to [https://drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com) (same account).

## Permission check before the workshop

If your work email is managed by your employer (Workspace admin), the IT policy might restrict third-party OAuth connections. Check this **before** the workshop:

1. Open Gmail.
2. Click your profile photo (top-right) → **Manage your Google Account**.
3. Go to **Security** → **Third-party apps with account access**.
4. If you see other AI tools or apps you've authorised before, OAuth is allowed for your account.
5. If it's locked down, talk to your IT admin or use a personal Google account for the workshop. You can connect a work account later if policy changes.

## What we'll connect on the day

- **Gmail** — for inbox triage and email drafting.
- **Google Calendar** — for meeting prep and scheduling.
- **Google Drive** — for file reads, brand brief, prompt libraries.

## Don't worry about

- Setting up a new Google account just for this — your existing one is fine.
- Sheets / Docs / Slides individually — Drive covers them.
- Gmail labels or filters — we don't change your existing setup.

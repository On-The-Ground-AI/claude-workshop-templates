# Claude Pro setup

> Time: ~10 minutes. You'll need: an email address, a credit/debit card.

Claude Pro is Anthropic's paid plan that unlocks higher rate limits, projects, and the Claude.ai connectors you'll use in CW-1. Workshop attendees get the first month gifted as a S$30 reimbursement after payment confirmation.

## Steps

1. Go to [https://claude.ai](https://claude.ai) and sign up with your work email.

   <!-- SCREENSHOT: claude-signup-step-1.png — the Claude.ai sign-up screen -->

2. Verify your email (one-time code).

3. Click your initials (top-right) → **Settings** → **Plan**.

   <!-- SCREENSHOT: claude-plan-screen.png — the Plan settings page with Pro/Max tiers -->

4. Select **Claude Pro** (~US$20/month). Enter your card details. Confirm.

5. Verify Pro is active: the top-left of any chat shows a model picker, and paid models are selectable. Don't check for a specific model name — Anthropic ships new ones regularly and any guide naming one goes stale within months.

## What Pro unlocks

- 5× the message limit of the free tier.
- **Projects** — persistent context across conversations. We'll use these heavily in CW-1.
- **Connectors** — Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, etc. CW-1 is built around these.
- File uploads up to 30 MB per file.

## Don't yet sign up for Max

Claude Max (tiered pricing, above Pro) is great if you're a heavy user, but you can decide that after CW-1. Pro is enough for everyone walking in. CW-2 attendees who prefer Max will get more out of it on the day — but Pro is fine for v1.

## Gift reimbursement

Your S$30 Claude Pro gift will be reimbursed via Stripe credit or PayNow transfer within 5 business days of your payment confirmation. T&Cs at [claudecodeworkshops.vercel.app/policies](https://claudecodeworkshops.vercel.app/policies).

## Troubleshooting

- **Card declined?** Most SG-issued cards work. If yours doesn't, try a different card or contact Anthropic support.
- **Email already exists?** Sign in instead — you can upgrade an existing account to Pro from the Plan page.

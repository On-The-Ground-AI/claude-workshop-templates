## 1. Who am I, in one paragraph?

Replace this with your one-paragraph answer. Cover: what you do, who you do it for, what's unusual about your background or approach, where you're based. Three to five sentences. Don't optimise for Google — write like you'd introduce yourself to a thoughtful stranger at a dinner.

**Example:** "I'm a freelance product strategist based in Singapore. I work with early-stage SaaS founders on positioning and pricing — the messy part between "we built it" and "people pay for it." I came out of two B2B startup exits in Sydney and now do this on my own. I take six clients at a time, max."

---

## 2. Who do I work with, and who don't I work with?

Specific is better. Two lists.

**I work with:**
- [Specific audience 1]
- [Specific audience 2]

**I don't work with:**
- [Specific anti-audience]
- [Specific anti-audience]

**Example:**
- I work with: B2B SaaS founders past US$100k ARR but before US$2M; agency owners hitting the founder-bottleneck wall.
- I don't work with: pre-revenue founders, e-commerce, anyone who needs "marketing" without a positioning problem to fix.

---

## 3. What do I sell, exactly?

List your services / products. Real names, real prices if you have them.

**Example:**
- 4-week positioning sprint — US$8,000
- Quarterly pricing review — US$2,500
- One-day strategy intensive — US$3,500 (Singapore in-person, online elsewhere)

**Example:** "By the end of the 4-week sprint, you can describe your product in 20 words in a way your sales team uses verbatim in calls."

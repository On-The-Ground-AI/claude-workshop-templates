## 1. What does the business do, in one paragraph?

Replace this with your one-paragraph answer. Three to five sentences. Cover: what you sell, who you sell it to, what's true about you that's not true about competitors, where you're based.

**Example:** "We're a remote-first transport SaaS for small SG logistics outfits. Our software replaces the WhatsApp + paper-receipt mess most family-owned lorry companies still run on. We're built by people who actually drove lorries in their first jobs. Singapore-headquartered, six people."
